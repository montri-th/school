# CityMETER School — owner preferences

Set 2026-11-05 (release 0.1.0) on the owner request “จากที่เราทำ demo ของ CityMETER: Hotel มาแล้ว ใน https://montri-th.github.io/hotel ขอให้ทำแบบเดียวกันให้กับ CityMETER: School แล้ว publish ที่ https://github.com/montri-th/school โดยใช้ข้อมูลจาก [the school research master] ซึ่งรวบรวมมาจาก งานเดิมใน production ที่เป็น CityMETER: School มาปรับปรุง ตาม source ของ https://landometer.com/v3/citymeter?d=school … update dataset registry sheet ด้วยนะ และพี่ช่วยสร้าง repo ใหม่ใน github ให้ด้วย”. “แบบเดียวกัน” = the CityMETER · Hotel 0.3.6 contract (code base montri-th/hotel @177e592), so every Hotel rule that is not about hotels applies here as written; hotel-only features (chain logos, recorded prices, meeting rooms, retail context, current brand names, resort sites) have no school counterpart and are not carried.

- Apply current LDS 0.9.7 (v0.9.7-owner.1, standalone-0.9.7-r1, color-srgb-10) and the Location Intelligence Profile for LDS 0.9.7 (`li.supply` for students), and preserve the map-first CityMETER layout. Colours come only from `assets/theme.css`, `js/scales.js` and `js/cues.js`; never approximate a value by eye. Analytical colours are the same HEX on both themes; EVID-05 map treatments use the `dataState` tokens. Colour scales always use the full 41 LDS samples (owner standing rule). `npm run check:lds -- --lds <base> --li <profile>` compares every shipped value with both source files.
- Read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues. The question is “นักเรียนกระจุกอยู่ตรงไหน และรอบแปลงมีโรงเรียนแบบไหน”.
- Measure is a contract (`js/model.js#METRICS`, `rowValue`): students recorded (Σ `student_count`, “คน”, `li.supply`) by default, records (“แห่ง”, `count`) and learning-service records (“เรียนเสริม”, “แห่ง”, `count`, 0.2.0) on the switch; the map, legend, labels, Aha, ranks and charts read the same measure. Unknown students are never 0: a record with unknown students is counted apart, a district whose records all have unknown students is `no_data` (hatch), a tile reads “—” (its title says why), and a chip, bar or row whose students are unknown reads in records or “ไม่ทราบจำนวนนักเรียน”. Never call a figure seats, capacity, demand, an enrolment rate, school-age population, a market share, a density or a ranking.
- Groups follow the source's affiliation and institution category, by stable id (`js/cues.js#GROUP_SERIES`, DATAVIZ-05 series + shape cue; no star). International = an accepted OPEC international record linked to the UUID (`15_RESEARCH_COVERAGE`, `16_ENROLMENT`), never a name containing “นานาชาติ”. Affiliations are the source's 16 codes; their counts reconcile with `01_AFFILIATIONS` at build time.
- Levels come from the source's grade codes only (สพฐ. schools in the 2026 source); every other record is “ไม่ทราบระดับชั้น” — a filter option, a hatched bar and a comparison rule without a level chip. Never infer a level from a name (อนุบาล…, …วิทยาคม).
- Tuition is published per school only, always “ค่าเรียนต่อปี … ปีการศึกษา <as the school wrote it> · ต่อคนต่อปี · ค่าเรียน”, from the school's own announcement; never summed, averaged across schools, called a price level or a cost of a year. A source the research team holds as a private copy (Drive) is never linked. A school without tuition in the data is not free.
- The ONESQA result is shown verbatim with its year and the open-data catalogue page; OPEC enrolment history per semester with “—” for a missing semester; a closure notice first, dated, with its link. None of them is a rank or a quality level.
- Aha first, in 3D: the first paint is the `city` preset framing up to three เขต/อำเภอ with the most of the measure in the province that holds the most of it at district level, focused automatically. Show the share of the province's whole total, numerator and denominator, the rail comparison as two bars (both bases in each bar's title and accessible text; hidden when nothing in the province is near an operating station) and the district(s) with the most outside that province. Never cut through a tie, never focus every district that holds the measure (`js/area-insight.js`). Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, never UI constants.
- Membership is the district the source lists the school under (`row.admin`). Never re-assign by point-in-polygon on display coordinates or by a name at runtime. A sheet point in another district or province is kept and reported (`meta.checks`).
- Points are the sheet's checked registry points (ONESQA or OPEC, `row.coordRole`), never an entrance or a campus; a row without one counts everywhere except as a pin.
- Context comes from datasets of the same registry, joined by id with the commit and SHA-256 recorded (`meta.context`): CityMETER · Transit stations (every operating station drawn as a quiet clickable ring; under construction named in words only, the construction pictogram and “กำลังสร้าง”). Every distance is a straight line between reference points — the distance pictogram, “ระยะตรง” in titles — never a walking route, a travel time, a school zone or a catchment.
- Comparison set (`js/profile-model.js#compsetOf`): same group, at least one shared level when the school's grades are known (else the group alone), within 2 km straight line, nearest first, every row of the dataset; headed “คู่เทียบ” with the rule as chips (type · levels · ≤ 2 กม.), with every record round the school beside it; never “คู่แข่ง” or a ranking.
- National scale: `data/area-index.json` has every district without geometry; outlines load per province (`js/frames.js`) for the selection and for the provinces under the map at district zoom (≤ 6 per move). Never load all 77 frame files up front.
- Focus + quiet context: detail (fills, coloured cue symbols, rounded focus massing) only for the focused part. Everything else stays visible but quiet. Below zoom 8 the province frames carry the reading — except with a จังหวัด filter.
- Symbol size (Hotel rule of 2026-11-01, point 4): the symbol's area grows with the recorded students (`js/model.js#pinRadius`, `PIN_SCALE`, cap `STUDENTS_SIZE_CAP`), the colour stays the group's; unknown students are an open (hollow) shape at the 200-student size; quiet context dots follow the same rule at about half size; larger symbols are drawn first. The legend key shows 100 · 500 · 2,000 คน and the hollow mark.
- Names near the middle of the view (`js/labels.js`): from zoom 12.5, focus rows in the central ellipse, ranked by `row.importance` (national percentiles of recorded students and published tuition; a missing value is never guessed), at most 9 on desktop and 5 on a phone; a label is the group cue, one short name (the leading โรงเรียน · รร. · ศพด. dropped; a place inside the name kept) and the students pictogram with the count; hover or focus opens a card of icon chips. No logos.
- Terrain (`js/terrain.js`) and the building view (`js/camera.js`, `js/massing.js`, `js/venue-xray.js`, `js/maplibre-guard.js`, MapLibre pinned at 5.6.2, `GEOJSON_MAXZOOM`) follow the Hotel 0.3.x contracts unchanged: choosing a school with a point switches the map to 3D and opens the two-point camera on the drawn building holding its pin, else the drawn building nearest the pin within 300 m (800 m once) — a camera choice only, never a join, a count or a located-in claim. Do not reorder the massing, x-ray, station and pin layers casually.
- Buildings per area: the units of the CityMETER · Hotel build (`data/building-units/`, copied by `scripts/adapt-building-units.py` from a named Hotel commit without the per-unit hotel counts); the source per unit is decided at build time from counts recorded in the file, never at runtime. The building panel says “สูงราว” and heads its list “สถานศึกษาในอาคารนี้”, a school “อีก N แห่งในอาคารนี้” — the pins in the drawn outline, never “ตั้งอยู่ใน”.
- One symbol always represents one row with a point; never cluster or aggregate. HTML pins from zoom 15.5 inside the focus only (≤ 320); quiet context dots are never focusable or clickable.
- Breadcrumb is ประเทศไทย › จังหวัด › เขต/อำเภอ with production codes and deep links `/v3/citymeter/{code}/D/{district}?d=school`. A school or a station is the panel heading, never a crumb.
- Every panel view ends with exactly one provenance line (`provenance()` in `js/panel.js`): dataset · source · date · boundary as pictogram-led parts, then “วิธีนับ” (DATA_METHOD.md); สช. (OPEC), สมศ. (ONESQA), the schools' fee announcements, rail and terrain join the source part when the view shows them. No caveat anywhere on screen (Hotel owner rule of 2026-11-01, “ไม่มี caveats เลย”): exactness comes from the names, the numerator and denominator of every share and the provenance line (`tests/views.test.js`).
- Copy (Hotel owner rule of 2026-11-01, “กระชับอ่านเข้าใจง่าย ใช้ symbol, logo, icon, charts … ตรงกับ context ของ ICP … ไม่มี caveats เลย และเป็นธรรมชาติ”): short, natural Thai in the ICP's words — นักเรียน / แห่ง / สถานศึกษา / โรงเรียน / ประเภท / สังกัด / ระดับชั้น / ขนาด / ค่าเรียน / ผลประเมิน / สถานี / เขต·อำเภอ / คู่เทียบ / รอบแปลง; pictograms from the approved subset (`scripts/build-pictogram-font.py`), small bar charts and chips carry what a sentence used to; sources, methods and ids behind one “ที่มาและพิกัด” disclosure; units on every number; a number and its unit never split across lines. No static counts in page copy (help, meta): numbers come from the data.
- Outbound actions (`js/ask.js`): ถาม Google AI with a question written for the view (a plain Google Search answered by its AI Overview, never a link into AI Mode), Google Maps search, Street View only where the view has a point. Public names and published points only. Nothing from Google's answers enters the data.
- Phone layout (Hotel 0.3.5): below 761 px in portrait the Aha line and the legend are one dock, the sheet is 56dvh and opens on the selection; verify at 390 px in light and dark.
- Privacy and publication: never copy official school codes as a field, record ids of the source, researcher notes, review queues, coordinate source URLs or raw source records (`meta.excludedFields`); source links are public https pages only, never the production API or a private copy. Never commit the research sheet, its cells or its address, private Drive/Sheet URLs, credentials, the full LDS library or the Location Intelligence Profile. Never put Landometer prices, contracts, proposals or pipeline in this repository. Review Thai at desktop and 390 px in light and dark before claiming done; local renders are not proof of a live release — run `npm run verify:live` and walk the live site.
- Releases bump one cache tag `?v=YYYYMMDD-rN` on every entry in `index.html` and, through the import map there, on every ES module, together with `package.json` version, `design-intent.json` and the handoff JSON `productVersion` (`tests/release.test.js`). A new module must be added to the import map. Icon fonts inside the stylesheets carry `?v=<first 8 hex of their SHA-256>` (`tests/icons.test.js`).
- Learning services (0.2.0; owner 2026-11-06: “เพิ่ม … 27_LEARNING_SERVICES และข้อมูลที่อัพเดทเพิ่มมาในชีทด้วย”, then chose the district level + the third measure + checked pins): `data/learning.json` from tabs 27–29 of the same master (`scripts/build-learning.py`, which stops when 27 ≠ 28 in scope + 29 provider-confirmed, when the list's summary differs from its rows or when a code is a school code). The measure `learning` reads these rows only — never add them to the school totals, never mix them into the school rows. Services overlap (a total counts a record once, a service bar counts it in each service). Membership is the register's DOPA district code; a pin only where the registry point lies inside the listed district (build time), every other record counts without one. Every learning record is LDS series 8 (ring), filled at the 200-student size; no star. The school, district and station panels of the school measures show the district's learning records by service and the pinned ones within 1 km (500 m round a station); a learning record shows the schools with a pin within 1 km. Crossing measures from a list keeps the step in the back stack with its measure (`js/app.js#openRow`). No OPEC code as a field, no address, no learner count or course price; “ยังเปิดสอน” is never claimed.
- Registry: the CityMETER Dataset Registry sheet row `research.TH.school` carries the demo columns (web, repository, checked date, check note) and a Changes row per release; edit those cells in place, never a new copy of the sheet.

## Discovery and dataset group (owner decision 2026-11-08)

- Every page carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`. The owner does not want search engines to index the demos while developers keep building on them: “ไม่ต้องการให้เสิช engine เข้ามาดูเดโม แต่อยากให้ dev นำ demo บน github ไป vibe coding ต่อได้ง่าย คือ SEO อันไหนจัดการได้จัดการไปเลย แต่ยังไม่ต้องให้ crawler เข้ามา index”. Keep the rest of the SEO metadata ready for production, do not block crawlers in a robots.txt (they must be able to read the noindex), add no sitemap, and never point `rel="canonical"` at landometer.com from a noindex page. `tests/discovery-and-group.test.js` guards this.
- The panel eyebrow `CITYMETER · LOCATION` follows the dataset registry (`R01_DATASETS.dataset_group`; owner: “ใช้ตามทะเบียนทั้งหมด”). This dataset is LOCATION in the registry read on 2026-11-08; change the label only when the registry changes.
