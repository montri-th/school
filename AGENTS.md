Analytical colours are the same HEX on both themes; EVID-05 map treatments use the `dataState` tokens. Colour scales always use the full 41 LDS samples (owner standing rule). `npm run check:lds -- --lds <base> --li <profile>` compares every shipped value with both source files.
- Read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues. The question is “นักเรียนกระจุกอยู่ตรงไหน และรอบแปลงมีโรงเรียนแบบไหน”.
- Measure is a contract (`js/model.js#METRICS`, `rowValue`): students recorded (Σ `student_count`, “คน”, `li.supply`) by default, records (“แห่ง”, `count`) and learning-service records (“เรียนเสริม”, “แห่ง”, `count`, 0.2.0) on the switch; the map, legend, labels, Aha, ranks and charts read the same measure. Unknown students are never 0: a record with unknown students is counted apart, a district whose records all have unknown students is `no_data` (hatch), a tile reads “—” (its title says why), and a chip, bar or row whose students are unknown reads in records or “ไม่ทราบจำนวนนักเรียน”. Never call a figure seats, capacity, demand, an enrolment rate, school-age population, a market share, a density or a ranking.
- Groups follow the source's affiliation and institution category, by stable id (`js/cues.js#GROUP_SERIES`, DATAVIZ-05 series + shape cue; no star). International = an accepted OPEC international record linked to the UUID (`15_RESEARCH_COVERAGE`, `16_ENROLMENT`), never a name containing “นานาชาติ”. Affiliations are the source's 16 codes; their counts reconcile with `01_AFFILIATIONS` at build time.
- Levels come from the source's grade codes only (สพฐ. schools in the 2026 source); every other record is “ไม่ทราบระดับชั้น” — a filter option, a hatched bar and a comparison rule without a level chip. Never infer a level from a name (อนุบาล…, …วิทยาคม).
- Tuition is published per school only, always “ค่าเรียนต่อปี … ปีการศึกษา <as the school wrote it> · ต่อคนต่อปี · ค่าเรียน”, from the school's own announcement; never summed, averaged across schools, called a price level or a cost of a year. A source the research team holds as a private copy (Drive) is never linked. A school without tuition in the data is not free.
- The ONESQA result is shown verbatim with its year and the open-data catalogue page; OPEC enrolment history per semester with “—” for a missing semester; a closure notice first, dated, with its link. None of them is a rank or a quality level.
- Aha first, in 3D: the first paint is the `city` preset framing up to three เขต/อำเภอ with the most of the measure in the province that holds the most of it at district level, focused automatically. Show the share of the province's whole total, numerator and denominator, the rail comparison as two bars (both bases in each bar's title and accessible text; hidden when nothing in the province is near an operating station) and the district(s) with the most outside that province. Never cut through a tie, never focus every district that holds the measure (`js/area-insight.js`). Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, never UI constants.
- Membership is the district the source lists the school under (`row.admin`). Never re-assign by point-in-polygon on display coordinates or by a name at runtime. A sheet point in another district or province is kept and reported (`meta.checks`).
- Points are the sheet's checked registry points (ONESQA or OPEC, `row.coordRole`), never an entrance or a campus; a row without one counts everywhere except as a pin.
- Context comes from datasets of the same registry, joined by id with the commit and SHA-256 recorded (`meta.context`): CityMETER · Transit stations (every operating station drawn as a quiet clickable ring; under construction named in words only, the construction pictogram and “กำลังสร้าง”). Every distance is a straight line between reference points — the distance pictogram, “ระยะตรง” in titles — never a walking route, a travel time, a school zone or a catchment.
- Comparison set (`js/profile-model.js#compsetOf`): same group, at least one shared level when the school's grades are known (else the group alone), within 2 km straight line, nearest first, every row of the dataset; headed “คู่เทียบ” with the rule as chips (type · levels · ≤ 2 กม.), with every record round the school beside it; never “คู่แข่ง” or a ranking.
- National scale: `data/area-index.json` has every district without geometry; outlines load per province (`js/frames.js`) for the selection and for the provinces under the map at district zoom (≤ 6 per move). Never load all 77 frame files up front.
- Focus + quiet context: detail (fills, coloured cue symbols, rounded focus massing) only for the focused part. Everything else stays visible but quiet. Below zoom 8 the province frames carry the reading — except with a จังหวัด filter.
- Symbol size (Hotel rule of 2026-11-01, point 4): the symbol's area grows with the recorded students (`js/model.js#pinRadius`, `PIN_SCALE`, cap `STUDENTS_SIZE_CAP`), the colour stays the group's; unknown students are an open (hollow) shape at the 200-student size; quiet context dots follow the same rule at about half size; larger symbols are drawn first. The legend key shows 100 · 500 · 2,000 คน and the hollow mark.
- Names near the middle of the view (`js/labels.js`): from zoom 12.5, focus rows in the central ellipse, ranked by `row.importance` (national percentiles of recorded students and published tuition; a missing value is never guessed), at most 9 on desktop and 5 on a phone; a label is the group cue, one short name (the leading โรงเรียน · รร. · ศพด. dropped; a place inside the name kept) and the students pictogram with the count; hover or focus opens a card of icon chips. No logos.
- Terrain (`js/shell/terrain.js`) and the building view (`js/shell/camera.js`, `js/massing.js`, `js/shell/venue-xray.js`, `js/shell/maplibre-guard.js`, MapLibre pinned at 5.6.2, `GEOJSON_MAXZOOM`) follow the Hotel 0.3.x contracts unchanged: choosing a school with a point switches the map to 3D and opens the two-point camera on the drawn building holding its pin, else the drawn building nearest the pin within 300 m (800 m once) — a camera choice only, never a join, a count or a located-in claim. - Buildings per area: the units of the CityMETER · Hotel build (`data/building-units/`, copied by `scripts/adapt-building-units.py` from a named Hotel commit without the per-unit hotel counts); the source per unit is decided at build time from counts recorded in the file, never at runtime. ## Search — names found the way people type them (owner 2026-11-12)

- Since release 0.7.0 (citymeter-shell 0.7.0; owner 2026-11-12 12:05, VERBATIM: “ศึกษาแนวคิดเรื่อง Fuzzy search แล้ว implement กับ demo web ให้ได้ user benefit สูงสุดตาม user intent ในขณะนั้น และตาม product statement”; 13:15 “รวม university ไว้ด้วยกันเลย”) the search box lists names instead of filtering while it is typed in. `js/shell/fuzzy.js` is the matcher — the method of the CityMETER area-search brief (02-CityMETER__fuzzy-search-tech-brief v1, 19 Oct 2026, revised 20 Oct), moved from PostgreSQL to the browser because a demo is a static site: a name as typed, without its tone marks, by letters that sound alike, with its lead word (จ. อ. เขต) or the place it is in, typed on the other keyboard layout, or one or two keys off; the last three are guesses, made only when the sure steps find little. `js/shell/search.js` is the list under the box (a WAI-ARIA combobox in the flow of the panel) and `js/find.js` gives it this demo's names: provinces, เขต/อำเภอ, affiliations, school types, learning services, schools, learning records and rail stations.
- Intent decides the action: a place opens its level, an affiliation or a school type its filter (on the school measures), a learning service its filter on the learning measure, a school or a learning record its profile on its own measure, a station the schools round it. Enter with nothing chosen applies the words as the text filter, as the box always did; when the words keep no row, Enter marks the first name found as typed and Enter again opens it — a row is never opened unmarked. The map and the panel do not change while the reader types.
- What is chosen is shown. A filter or words chosen from the list apply to the whole page: the place being looked at is left (the province stays — a province without the brand is a reading of that province) and the other filters that would leave the choice no row are lifted first, the fewest that do (`makeWay` → `filtersToLift`, js/shell/search.js). An item chosen always opens: the filters it does not pass are lifted (`makeWayFor` → `filtersAgainst`). The `filters` option of `installSearch` (js/app.js) names the filters that may be lifted — never a counting unit or a measure. The number on the list's last row is the rows the words keep with the filters that are on — or, when those leave them no row, with the ones that stay once the fewest are lifted, as choosing the row does (`rowsAfterWay`).
- A guess is never chosen for the reader (the brief's decision of 10 Oct 2026: “เลือกแถวให้อัตโนมัติเฉพาะเมื่อมีพื้นที่เดียวที่ชื่อตรง”). Only an entry that alone is written with the very name typed, or that name with its place, is marked ahead — never a school, and never an entry that only goes by the name once its head word is left out (“ภูเก็ต” is the province; เทศบาลนครภูเก็ต is found by it and is a row below).
- A row says what an entry is and where, in words: no number and no caveat. How sure a match is stands in words too — the tag “สะกดต่างจากที่พิมพ์”, the heading “ชื่อใกล้เคียง”.
- Nothing typed is stored or sent: no query log and no feedback button (the brief keeps both for after its PDPA review).
